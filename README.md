# hdlbits-solutions

158 HDLBits problems solved from scratch between 1 May and 14 June 2026. Covers
nearly the whole problem set: the language sections, combinational and
sequential circuits, shift registers, the full FSM section including the
exam-style Q-series, and testbench writing.

Two sections I skipped deliberately — Karnaugh Map to Circuit and Cellular
Automata — since I was after RTL fluency rather than manual logic minimization
or puzzle circuits. Four FSM problems are unfinished. Everything committed here
is a solution I completed and can explain.

## Sections
- `01-getting-started`
- `02-verilog-language` — wires, vectors, modules, procedures
- `03-circuits` — combinational logic, sequential logic, shift registers, FSMs
- `04-verification-reading-simulations` — reading waveforms, inferring circuits
- `05-writing-testbenches`
